What you get

👤

Profiles & social graph

Register, customize your profile with avatar, cover, bio, status and city. Find friends by username and build your circle.

📰

Feed & posts

Ranked feed with text and images. Like, comment, repost, react with emojis, and save posts for later.

📸

Stories

Share 24-hour photo stories with friends. See what they're up to, right now.

💬

Messages & group chats

Direct messages and group chats with end-to-end encryption. Pin, mute, unsend, draft, react, search — all there.

📞

Audio & video calls

One-on-one calls with WebRTC. Clear audio, smooth video, right inside the app.

🌐

Communities

Create or join communities with their own roles, posting permissions, and invite links.

💎

UX currency

Earn UX for activity, gift it to friends, or buy it via Telegram bot. Admins can grant or deduct from the control panel.

🔐

E2EE everywhere

Chat messages and attachments are encrypted end-to-end. The server never sees your private conversations.

Everywhere you are

Android

Native Kotlin / Jetpack Compose app. Liquid glass UI, E2EE, calls, stories, and the full social experience.

Web

Full-featured SPA served by the API or hosted on GitHub Pages. Same features as Android, right in your browser.

Desktop

Tauri, Electron, and Python/Qt clients for Windows, Linux, and macOS. All wrap the same web experience.
