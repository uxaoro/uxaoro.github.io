UXAORO — PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: September 24, 2026
Last updated:   September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Uxaoro collects, why, who
it is shared with, how long it is kept, and what choices you have. It
applies to the Uxaoro social network operated by fourtexec ("Uxaoro", "we",
"us"): the service at uxaoro.fourtexec.site and api.uxaoro.fourtexec.site,
the web client, the Android app, the desktop apps (Windows, Linux, macOS),
the Uxaoro Telegram bot and the "Login with Uxaoro" developer platform
(together, the "Service"). Words defined in our Terms of Use
(https://uxaoro.fourtexec.site/tou.txt) have the same meaning here.

fourtexec is the controller of your personal data. Contact:
support@fourtexec.site.

Short version:
  * We do not show ads, do not sell your data, and do not use third-party
    analytics or tracking SDKs in our apps or website.
  * Your chat messages and chat attachments are end-to-end encrypted. We
    store only ciphertext and cannot read them.
  * Your profile, posts, stories, comments, reactions and the files attached
    to them are public or visible to other users by design.
  * Public posts are checked by an AI moderation provider for adult content.
  * We share data only with the service providers listed in Section 5, with
    apps you explicitly authorise, or when the law requires it.


1. DATA YOU GIVE US

1.1. Account data
     * Username, email address, display name (initially your username).
     * Password — stored only as a salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hash; we never store
       or see it in plain text.
     * The email verification code we send you (stored hashed, expires).
     * Optional two-factor authentication secret (TOTP) if you enable 2FA.

1.2. Profile data (optional)
     Bio, status, city (only what you type — we do not track your location),
     avatar, cover image, Premium customisation (glow, name colour and
     style, badge, chat bubble and profile theme), and your settings: privacy
     options (who can message you, call you, see your last-seen time, see
     you online, send friend requests, invite you to communities or groups,
     see your stories), "Show NSFW content" and "My account is NSFW".

1.3. Content and social activity
     * Posts, reposts, comments, likes, emoji reactions, saved posts.
     * Photos, videos and audio you upload to posts, stories, avatars,
       covers and communities. Files are checked by type and size; we do not
       analyse faces or build biometric profiles.
     * Stories (shown for 24 hours) and the list of users who viewed them.
     * Posts you have viewed (recorded once per post, to credit UX rewards
       and prevent duplicate rewards).
     * Friends and friend requests, communities you created or joined and
       your role in them, group chats and roles, blocked users.
     * Sticker packs and stickers you create.
     * Reports you file (target, reason, details).

1.4. Messages
     Messages and attachments are encrypted on your device with a random
     AES-256-GCM key, which is wrapped with the RSA public key of each chat
     member. Our servers store:
     * the encrypted message and encrypted attachments (as opaque files),
       nonces and wrapped keys — we cannot decrypt them;
     * your RSA public key (the private key never leaves your device);
     * metadata that is needed to deliver messages and is NOT encrypted:
       which chats exist and who is a member, group titles and roles, who
       sent a message and when, message type, sticker used, reactions to
       messages, read position (for read receipts), pinned and muted chats,
       and whether a message was deleted.

1.5. Calls
     Calls are peer-to-peer WebRTC connections, encrypted in transit
     (DTLS-SRTP). Our server only relays the call set-up signals (invite,
     offer, answer, network candidates, hang-up) between the two devices in
     real time; it does not record or store the audio, video or those
     signals. To connect a call, your device exchanges network addresses
     (including your IP address) with the other person's device and contacts
     public STUN servers (by default operated by Google). If a TURN relay is
     configured, encrypted call media may pass through it.

1.6. UX, Premium and payments
     * UX balance and the history of UX movements (gifts sent and received,
       including the optional gift message, purchases, activity rewards and
       administrator grants/deductions with their reason).
     * Purchases: invoice ID, amount of UX, price and currency, status and
       time. Payment itself happens in Telegram through CryptoBot (Crypto
       Pay); we never receive your wallet keys, seed phrase or card data.
     * If you link the Telegram bot: your Telegram user ID and Telegram
       username, and the temporary link code.
     * Premium status and its expiry date.

1.7. Developer platform
     * Developer status, verification codes (hashed), projects (name,
       description, website, redirect URIs), API keys (stored hashed; only a
       short prefix is kept readable).
     * "Login with Uxaoro": which apps you authorised, authorisation codes
       and access tokens (stored hashed), their scope and expiry.

1.8. Support
     Emails you send to support@fourtexec.site and our replies.


2. DATA COLLECTED AUTOMATICALLY

2.1. Sessions: access tokens are signed and expire; refresh tokens are
     stored hashed with their expiry and revocation status.
2.2. Presence: the time you were last active and whether you are online
     (shown to others according to your privacy settings).
2.3. Notifications we create for you (likes, comments, friend requests,
     gifts, etc.) and whether you have read them.
2.4. Network data: your IP address is used in memory to enforce rate limits
     (by default 20 requests per second per endpoint) and is not written to
     our database. Our web server and reverse proxy keep standard technical
     logs (IP address, time, requested URL, status, user agent) for security
     and troubleshooting; these logs are rotated and kept for a limited
     period (normally no longer than 30 days) unless needed to investigate
     abuse.
2.5. Update checks: the apps request a small manifest from
     https://fourtexec.site/downloads/uxaoro/upd/latest.json and, if you
     choose, download the installer from the same place. These requests
     contain no account data; the web server sees the IP address and user
     agent as with any web request.
2.6. We do not use advertising identifiers, cookies for tracking, or
     third-party analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.


3. DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

The apps store on your device: your session tokens, your E2EE private key
(Android: app-private storage; web and desktop: browser / web-view local
storage), an offline cache of feeds, profiles and decrypted chats so you can
read them without a connection, message drafts, appearance settings, and the
update "remind me later" time. Downloaded update installers are saved to
your Downloads folder. Logging out clears the session; uninstalling the app
or clearing browser data removes the rest — export your private key in
Settings first if you want to keep reading old messages on a new device.

Device permissions: the Android app asks for the camera and microphone only
when you make a call or record/take media, and for "install unknown apps"
only when you choose to install an update. It does not access your
contacts, location, SMS or call log.

 a) To provide the Service — accounts, profiles, feed, posts, stories,
    friends, communities, messaging, calls, notifications, UX, Premium,
    developer platform. (Performance of our contract with you.)
 b) To rank your feed — using your friends, communities and interactions to
    choose which posts to show first. We do not build advertising profiles.
    (Contract; legitimate interest in a useful feed.)
 c) To keep Uxaoro safe — email verification, 2FA, rate limiting, spam and
    fraud prevention, handling reports, enforcing the Terms, keeping an
    audit log of staff actions. (Legitimate interests; legal obligations.)
 d) Automated content moderation — see Section 6. (Legitimate interest in
    protecting users from unwanted adult content and in complying with the
    law.)
 e) To communicate with you — verification codes, security and service
    messages, replies to support requests. We do not send marketing emails.
    (Contract; legitimate interests.)
 f) To comply with the law and respond to lawful requests. (Legal
    obligation.)
 g) Where we rely on consent (for example linking Telegram, authorising a
    third-party app, turning on "Show NSFW content"), you can withdraw it at
    any time in the app.


5. WHO WE SHARE DATA WITH

5.1. Other users — see Section 7 for what is visible.
5.2. Service providers that process data for us:
     * Resend (email delivery) — your email address and the content of
       verification emails.
     * Our AI moderation provider (an OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible
       model API) — public post content, see Section 6.
     * Telegram — if you use the Uxaoro bot, your messages with the bot are
       handled by Telegram.
     * CryptoBot / Crypto Pay — invoice data when you buy UX (amount, asset,
       invoice description). Your payment is made in your own Telegram /
       CryptoBot account under their privacy policy.
     * STUN (and, if configured, TURN) servers — your IP address during call
       set-up, see Section 1.5.
     * Our hosting / server provider, which stores the database and files.
5.3. Apps you authorise with "Login with Uxaoro" receive your user ID,
     username, display name and avatar URL — nothing else — until you revoke
     access (Developer -> Granted access).
5.4. Legal reasons — we may disclose data if we believe in good faith that
     the law requires it, or that it is necessary to protect the life or
     safety of a person, to investigate fraud or abuse, or to defend our
     rights. End-to-end encrypted content cannot be disclosed because we do
     not have it in readable form.
5.5. Business transfer — if Uxaoro is transferred to another operator, your
     data may be transferred with it under this Policy, and we will notify
     you.
5.6. We do not sell or rent personal data and do not share it for
     advertising.


6. AI CONTENT MODERATION

About once an hour, an automated moderator reviews new posts that have not
been checked yet. For each post it sends to our AI provider: the author's
username and display name, the community (if any), the post text,
attachment type/metadata and up to two of the post's photos (photos only
from our own storage, never fetched from other websites). The model returns whether the post is
adult (NSFW) content, a confidence score, categories and a short reason,
which we store on the post and in a moderation log. Posts are flagged, not
deleted: flagged posts are hidden from users who have not turned on "Show
NSFW content". An account where, within 7 days, at least half of its posts
(and at least 4 posts) are NSFW receives an NSFW mark that only staff can
remove. Staff can review and override every automated decision; contact
support@fourtexec.site to ask for a human review.

Private messages are never sent to the AI provider — they are end-to-end
encrypted and we cannot read them.


7. WHAT OTHER PEOPLE CAN SEE

Visible to other users (and, through the API, to anyone signed in):
 * username, display name, bio, status, city, avatar, cover, role (e.g.
   admin, moderator), registration date, number of friends, developer
   badge, Premium status and customisation;
 * your UX balance;
 * your account NSFW mark and the share of your posts marked NSFW;
 * your posts, reposts, comments, likes, reactions, stories (to the
   audience allowed by "Who can see my stories"), community memberships and
   roles;
 * your last-seen time and online status, according to your settings.
Files attached to posts, stories, avatars and covers are served from web
addresses that anyone who has the link can open, even without an account.

Not visible to other users: your email address, password hash, 2FA secret,
settings, saved posts, blocked users, reports you filed, purchase history,
Telegram link, and the content of chats you are not a member of.


8. HOW LONG WE KEEP DATA

 * Account and profile data: while your account exists.
 * Posts, comments, messages: until you delete them or your account. When
   you delete an item it is hidden from everyone immediately; the record may
   remain in our database in deleted state and is purged together with your
   account or during periodic clean-up.
 * Stories: hidden after 24 hours; their files may remain on the server
   until periodic clean-up or account deletion.
 * Pending registrations, verification codes, link codes and OAuth codes:
   until they expire (minutes to hours) or are used.
 * UX transactions and purchase records: for as long as your account
   exists, and afterwards for as long as needed for accounting, fraud
   prevention and legal obligations.
 * Moderation verdicts, reports and the staff audit log: as long as needed
   to enforce the Terms and handle appeals.
 * Server technical logs: see Section 2.4.
 * Backups: overwritten on a rolling basis.

Account deletion: email support@fourtexec.site from the address linked to
your account. We will verify the request and, within 30 days, delete your
account, profile, posts, comments, stories, reactions, uploaded files and
your copies of messages, or anonymise them where deletion is not possible
(for example, records we must keep by law, or messages that remain in other
members' chats in encrypted form). Content other users reshared may remain
as a reference without your data.


9. SECURITY

We protect data with HTTPS/TLS for all traffic, end-to-end encryption of
messages and chat files, salted PBKDF2 password hashing, hashed tokens and
API keys, optional two-factor authentication, rate limiting, WebSocket
authentication that keeps tokens out of URLs, and (in the Android app)
protection against TLS-intercepting proxies. No system is perfectly secure;
if we learn of a breach that affects your personal data, we will notify you
and the authorities where the law requires it.


10. YOUR RIGHTS AND CHOICES

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/EEA/UK,
the CCPA/CPRA in California, or Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ "On Personal
Data"), you may have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it,
receive a copy in a portable format, restrict or object to processing, and
withdraw consent. Many of these you can do yourself in the app:
 * edit your profile, change privacy settings, change your password;
 * delete posts, comments, stories and messages;
 * turn "Show NSFW content" and "My account is NSFW" on or off (unless the
   NSFW mark is locked);
 * revoke apps you authorised, block users;
 * export your E2EE private key.
For anything else — a copy of your data, account deletion, objections —
email support@fourtexec.site from the address linked to your account. We
answer within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local
data-protection authority.

We do not sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioural
advertising as those terms are defined in the CCPA/CPRA.


11. CHILDREN

Uxaoro is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an
account, contact support@fourtexec.site and we will delete it. Adult content
is hidden by default, and users under 18 must not enable it (see the Terms
of Use).


12. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

Our servers and some of our providers (for example email delivery, AI
moderation, Telegram and CryptoBot) may be located in countries other than
yours, which may have different data-protection laws. Where required, we
rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers (such as standard
contractual clauses offered by the provider).
